## Profile

![Martijn Hemeryck](/about/avatar-160.png)

I am an experienced software engineer who designs, simplifies, and finishes practical systems.
My background spans research, signal processing, backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, and software architecture.
I work best where engineering requires both implementation and judgement: understanding the problem, defining boundaries, making tradeoffs, and carrying systems through to production.

## Engineering Focus

- Designing backend and integration systems that remain understandable over time.
- Working across application code, infrastructure, data, operations, and documentation.
- Turning ambiguous technical problems into maintainable implementations.
- Improving existing systems without unnecessary rewrites.
- Communicating architectural decisions and tradeoffs clearly.
